## TL;DR {#tl-dr}
This case study is in progress. It comes from the Multisensory Interactive Systems course at the University of Trento. In the lab exercises I work with Arduino, Teensy, sensors and Pure Data: for example, force, flex and proximity sensors that drive an RGB LED, and a low-pass filter on a Teensy that smooths accelerometer data. Our group's final project is just starting, and I'll update this page as it takes shape.
